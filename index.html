About Mark

Business owner and tech CFO for most of his career.

In 2023 he co-founded the Oceanbags Foundation with his wife Toni and Zane Haas. Their bags have gone to the Thailand–Myanmar border, the jungles of India, Mexico border towns and Central America, and their Teacher Totes put storybooks in Title I classrooms. Mark and Toni take no pay.

He's active in missions at Riverbend Church in Austin.
